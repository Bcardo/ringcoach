# Food portion corrections and submission status

Incident [#77](https://github.com/Bcardo/ringcoach/issues/77), under [#52](https://github.com/Bcardo/ringcoach/issues/52), exposed a semantic failure in a completed incremental meal update. The returned food retained its ID and acquired the corrected mass and a new composition explanation, but all nutrients exactly matched the preceding estimate. Source/revision validation and local arithmetic both passed. A completed queue therefore did not establish that the correction was understood. The precise internal reason for the model's copied values is unobserved.

The same ordinary submission separately recorded two nutrition-stage `network` errors and scheduled retries before automatic completion. Existing diagnostics cannot establish connection failure versus timeout. Those retries explain delayed completion but are not established as the cause of the incorrect estimate. No owner data is included in this incident record.

`nutrition-v2-portion-corrections` explicitly requires re-estimation of the affected food when a whole-meal mass is corrected, while preserving unrelated food facts and avoiding a duplicate meal or duplicated default breakfast. A narrow deterministic guard detects a materially changed, single explicit mass in an existing food's portion with an exactly unchanged nutrient map. Equivalent gram/kilogram expressions do not count as changed mass. The existing one-response repair applies; a second rejection blocks with `nutrition_portion_not_recalculated`. Nutrition diagnostic events now carry the nutrition prompt version rather than the coaching version.

This does not compute a food's true nutrition or mechanically scale a mixed meal. Ambiguous/multiple masses, changed composition, some-but-not-all stale nutrients and coincidentally changed wrong values remain outside the narrow guard. A genuine mass change with unchanged nutrition, such as added water, can require clarification rather than silent acceptance. Model estimates remain approximate. Continue ordinary-use acceptance for corrections and reopen/retry behavior; neither this guard nor a successful recovery proves general recognition reliability.

Submission status now appears above the daily panel, names recognition/coaching stages and automatic retries, shows a saved-advice timestamp after completion and offers a link from Journal to today's advice. Saving a current-date submission opens Today after the local commit; it does not claim the asynchronous advice is complete. Pending states explicitly say records are saved and should not be resubmitted. A read failure has a refresh action. Full daily notes and training advice are always displayed without ellipsis, duplicate compact excerpts or a collapse control, as requested by the owner. Historical submission routing remains unchanged.

Production source `0cd7d13` installed with a matching reviewed APK. Build, test APK, lint and 121 JVM tests passed. New regressions cover stale-mass rejection, changed estimates, equivalent units, one corrective response and preservation of unrelated foods. Eighteen synthetic physical date/processing checks passed through the required no-restart wrapper, including two intake network failures followed by completion without resubmission.

Explicit recovery reconstructed the original correction against the preceding converted state and required the saved stale-mass failure before proceeding. It used converted food evidence, not a re-uploaded old photo, kept the corrected portion and all other saved foods/supplements/breakfast handling, and atomically updated the affected current nutrition result. A separate new advice card used refreshed saved facts and labeled retained health context. The original journal, submission identities, plans, workouts, photos, other dates and all old cards were preserved. A post-recovery private audit confirmed only one food changed, one new card, matching coaching inclusion, database integrity and no pending queue. Installation preserved exact legacy accessibility configuration/preferences/binding and Ring opt-in. No fresh Bluetooth reception or new-night reliability is claimed.

The opt-in recovery tool is guarded for the specific two-turn completed-state class and is not an automatic product path. Do not rerun a successful repair. Current installed UI checks and merge/deployment status are recorded in the linked issue; ordinary-use reliability remains open.
