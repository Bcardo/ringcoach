# Numbered workout reports and dated advice

An ordinary-use report exposed a semantic recognition failure: the submission and its coach response were saved, but the completed incremental recognition contained unresolved evidence and no confirmed exercise rows. Training therefore had no new actuals to show. The following day's separately generated card continued discussing that workout. No provider/validation error was recorded; this is not a stuck queue or evidence that the source input was lost. The precise reason the model chose unresolved items is unobserved.

`workout-v4-numbered-reports` clarifies Day-number matching across spacing/case and descriptive labels, implicit actual reports with numbered performance lines, verbatim unitless performance, explicit skipped exercises, and separation from future plans. Missing units or effort do not justify inventing them or discarding otherwise identifiable reported performance. Evidence validation and bounded repair are unchanged. This remains model-based recognition; the added instructions do not guarantee arbitrary shorthand is understood.

`coach-v19-dated-training` explicitly separates prior-date reports from today's selection. Unresolved recognition is not proof that a workout was unperformed. Today's explicit swim/rest selection and applicable saved rules take precedence over repeating yesterday's session; flexible workout selection is retained and no automatic calendar rotation is introduced. Historical cards retain their original prompt version.

Two opted-in synthetic live tests exercise actual unitless numbered reports with a skipped exercise, future-plan exclusion, and next-day swim/rest advice. They use the candidate test-APK prompts, synthetic facts and the existing provider credential, without persisting responses or owner inputs. Build, lint and all 119 JVM tests pass. Finite tests and a manual repair do not establish ordinary-use recurrence acceptance.

The explicit owner recovery tool only accepts a day with one completed turn, no confirmed rows and nonempty unresolved evidence. It reuses the original submission, requires all previously unresolved evidence to be covered and no unresolved result, then checks source/state equality before an atomic replacement through the existing incremental store. It never creates a duplicate submission, alters the journal or saved plan, or replays nutrition. Ambiguous or changed input leaves the original state unchanged. It requires a separate opt-in and date, and never runs automatically.
